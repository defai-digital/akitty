# AkiTTY 2.1.4

- Preserve following text when an inserted grapheme grows to two columns,
  including when it wraps to the next row.
- Keep wide glyphs paired with their own trailing cells after character deletes.
- Preserve visible text written at a wrap boundary in selection, resize reflow
  and browser copying.
- Keep pending grapheme joins when a resize reports the current dimensions.

The native app requires Apple Silicon and macOS 27.2 or later.
