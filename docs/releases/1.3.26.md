# AkiTTY 1.3.26

## Improvements and fixes

- Avoid refilling rows that are already blank when clearing the screen or erasing a full row. Writes, resizing and background-color changes keep the blank-row state valid.
- Avoid wide-cell repair for ordinary single-column writes over plain cells, while retaining repair when overwriting either half of a wide glyph.
- Skip per-cell heap scans when adding plain rows to scrollback. Rows with combining marks or hyperlinks retain heap accounting, including after resize and reflow.
- Corrected the human-readable WASM provenance hash after the engine rebuilds. Verification now rejects a provenance document that omits the committed artifact's exact SHA-256.

## Verification

- The VT suite covers blank-row invalidation, background-color erases, alternate-screen cycles, wide-cell overwrite repair, history heap accounting, resize/reflow, and randomized grid invariants.
- Reproduced the stale-document failure before correcting the hash. The complete release gate covers Rust, native ABI/PTY and Swift behavior, TypeScript/Web browser behavior, rebuilt and committed WASM, benchmark smoke checks, shell integration, installer/release tooling and public-file hygiene.
